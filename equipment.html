Specs and fit

What we check before recommending anything

Machine choice is not about the biggest spec sheet. It is about your headcount, your peak hours, your counter space and your water.

We survey before we recommend. Water hardness decides whether you need softening and how often we service. Peak demand decides whether one machine is enough or you need a second on another floor.

Then we match the kit to the room, install it, calibrate it and show your team how to get the best from it.

Water survey

Hardness and pressure tested on site. The right filter set up correctly is the difference between good coffee and great coffee.

Counter planning

We started because the breakroom deserved better

Fresh & Reddy exists for one reason: to make bad office coffee a thing of the past. We roast, deliver, install and service so your team never has to think about it.

Our story

Built by people who got tired of apologising for the coffee

It began in a shared office in 2016. Eight desks, one ancient filter machine, and a rota nobody wanted to be on. The coffee was an afterthought, and it showed in every Monday morning.

We started dialling in better beans, then better machines, then a proper delivery schedule. Word travelled down the corridor and across the business park. Within a year we were supplying eleven offices.

Today we run full pantry programmes for offices of every size, from ten-person studios to four-hundred seat campuses. Same obsession with the cup, just more cups.

What we stand for

Four things we refuse to compromise on

Freshness first

Beans are roasted in small batches and delivered within seven days of roast. Nothing sits in a warehouse getting stale.

Full accountability

One team owns the machine, the beans, the water and the clean-up. You get one point of contact, not five.

Properly dialled in

Every install is calibrated to your water hardness and your people's taste. Great beans in a badly set machine still taste bad.

Real human support

Machine down before a board meeting? Call us and a person answers. Engineer on site within one working day.

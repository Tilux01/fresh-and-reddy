Good coffee should not cost the earth

We buy from farms we have visited, roast in batches we can trace, and pack in materials that actually break down. None of it is a marketing line.

Sourcing

We know where every bag comes from

Every coffee we sell is bought directly from the farm or through a co-operative we have a relationship with. We pay above the commodity price as standard because stable farmers grow better coffee.

We publish the farm, the region and the price we paid for every lot on your invoice. If you want to know what your office coffee funds, it is printed on the bag.

Twelve roast partners, five countries, one standard. If a harvest does not meet it, we do not sell it, even if that means a shortfall in a busy quarter.

Direct trade

Long-term relationships with named farms. Prices set above commodity and reviewed every harvest.

Traceable to the bag

Farm, altitude, process and roast date printed on every batch that leaves our roastery.

Fair work

Everyone in our supply chain, from picker to packer to driver, is paid properly and on time.

Community

Coffee is better when more people drink it well

Barista apprenticeships

We fund paid apprenticeships for young people leaving care and entering hospitality, with a guaranteed interview at the end.

Community nights

Free monthly brewing sessions at our roastery. Anyone can come, learn and take beans home.

Grounds programme

Spent grounds go to community gardens and allotments instead of the bin. We collect and deliver them ourselves.

Work with us

We are almost always hiring good people

If you care about the details, we probably want to talk. Drivers, engineers, roasters and account managers all start on the floor, learning how the whole operation fits together before specialising.

We run a four-day week for the roastery and service teams, pay above the local hospitality average, and close between Christmas and New Year.

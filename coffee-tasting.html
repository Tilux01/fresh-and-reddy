The session

A proper tasting, not a sales demo

A tasting takes about forty minutes from setup to pack-down. We arrive before the morning rush, set up a brewing station in a spare meeting room or the pantry, and run your team through a flight of four to six coffees.

We talk about origin, roast level and body in plain language. No jargon, no pressure. Everyone gets a card, marks what they like and we tally the results on the spot.

By the end you have a clear favourite, a runner-up for variety, and a recommendation on machine settings to get the best from both.
